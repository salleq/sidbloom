## SIDbloom 0.5.0

**New in 0.5.0**
- **Export to MP3** (`.mp3` button): the song is rendered once through (to the point where it would loop, plus a short fade-out) with the chosen SID chip and saved as a mono 128 kbit/s MP3 - ready to share or listen to anywhere. Songs longer than 10 minutes are cut off.

Also new since 0.4.0 (0.4.1): Mute / Solo per voice, copy / paste of instruments by part.
