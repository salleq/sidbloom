## SIDbloom 0.5.1

**New in 0.5.1**
- **Space** plays the song from the current pattern, **Ctrl+Space** loops the pattern, **Shift+Space** plays from the top. Buttons: **▶ Play**, **↻ Pattern**, **▶ Song**.
- Double-clicking a position in the song order during song playback continues the song from there.
- **Song order drag and drop:** drag to move a position (also to the end), Ctrl / Alt + drag to copy, and **Copy to end**.

Also new since 0.4.1 (0.5.0): export to MP3.
